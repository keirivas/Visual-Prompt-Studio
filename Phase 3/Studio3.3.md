Title: The Many Faces of Beauty

Prompt: Create a promotional landing page a contemporary art installation called, "The Many Faces of Beauty" for the theme Tradition and Transformation. Show a Greek marble statue that is diverse and modern and represents identity and expression. Mix realistic Greek sculpture with modern art. Try not to make it look too digital, make it look as realistic as possible but with modern art. Mix in fauvism, use those colors to also represent identity and emotions. Use regular lines to represent perfection and use irregular lines to represent individuality. Use bright colors against deep shadows to make the artwork stand out. Contrast the white marble with colorful modern figures to show the difference between traditional beauty and individual beauty. Include man and woman.

Vocab-
Contemporary Art: Art that shows emotions and ideas.
 Fauvism: Art style that uses plenty of color to express things.
 Directional Lines: Lines that guide.
 Value Contrast: Light and dark shades in an artwork.

Mashup: Greek Idealism and Identity + Self-Expression
