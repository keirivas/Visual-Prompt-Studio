Prompt A (painting): Create an artwork that uses oil painting and emphasizes emphasis, movement, texture, and lines. Use oil paint, show the thick layers, brushstrokes, blending, and slow drying paint (make it still look wet). The composition should feature a beautiful young woman with long flowing hair and a young man, paint in the style of a romantic 1800s painting. Give them soft, loving and calm expressions. Give them elegant clothing and a peaceful outdoor area with a willow tree in the back. Use the flowing hair, clothing to create the movement. Make the two people the emphasis. 
Prompt B (lithography): Create an artwork that uses printmaking, specifically lithography. It emphasizes the emphasis, shows movement, texture, and lines. Use a limestone, grease pencil, tusche, and printing press. The visual should feature a beautiful young woman with long flowing hair and a young man in a romantic 1800s style, with soft, loving expressions. They are wearing elegant clothing. Use the detailed lines and textures to create movement. add the willow tree in the back ans show movement in it as well.
