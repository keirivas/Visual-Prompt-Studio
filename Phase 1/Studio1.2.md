Visual Prompt Studio 1.2: The Immersive Album Cover Challenge
Wild and Free
Create a realistic Americana album cover with a blonde woman standing in the countryside. Make it look vintage. Shes looking towards the camera but looking to the side. She is wearing a vintage American style shirt with the flag on it. Behind her there is a red classic truck and a USA flag waving behind her. Have there be a road behind her and tress at a distance. Make it golden hour, use strong value and light by having sunlight around the girl and some parts of the lanscape but then the rest a little darker for shadows. Show the time through the sunset but also through the truck and vintage feel. Show motion with the woman's hair and the flag swaying. Make grass a little tall so that it also looks like its swaying. Make sure the girl is the closest/ largest. then the truck behind her and then everything else smaller. Use the same americana colors and repeating texture throughout the visual. Scale and proportion accurately shown by the sizes of woman and truck. Make it feel cinematic and realistic. Add space in the top right where there could possibly the name of the album and the artist. Name is something like Wild and free
Vocabulary Breakdown: Value- The light and dark parts in the visual. Space- The area around objects. Unity- How everything makes sense together. Balance- How the visual feels balanced. Rhythm- Elements that create a flow.
